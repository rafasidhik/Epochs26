# Northwind SQL Analysis

## Database Overview

The Northwind database is a sample business database containing information about customers, orders, products, categories, suppliers, and employees.

## Business Questions

- Top 10 Selling Products
- Top 10 Customers by Revenue
- Monthly Sales Trends
- Best-Performing Product Categories
- Customer Purchase Frequency

## Tools Used

- SQL (SQLite)
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Google Colab

## SQL Output

The SQL queries are available in the `queries.sql` file, and their outputs are shown in the `analysis.ipynb` notebook.

## Key Business Insights

1. A few products account for a large portion of total sales.
2. Top customers generate a significant share of revenue.
3. Sales vary across different months.
4. Some product categories perform better than others.
5. Repeat customers place orders more frequently.
